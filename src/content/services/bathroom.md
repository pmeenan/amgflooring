Your bathroom should be a sanctuary. AMG Flooring LLC applies our high standards of precision from the floor up, offering comprehensive bathroom remodeling services in Northern Virginia.

### Custom Tile Work
Our master installers specialize in intricate tile designs, mosaics, large-format porcelain, and natural stone. We ensure perfect slopes in shower pans and flawless waterproofing systems (like Schluter-Kerdi) to protect your home.

### Complete Renovations
We handle the entire process:
- Demolition and careful removal of old fixtures.
- Custom walk-in showers with niches and benches.
- Radiant heated flooring systems.
- Installation of vanities, mirrors, and fixtures.
- Upgraded lighting and ventilation.

Trust the 20+ years of expertise from Armend Gashi to build a bathroom that is as durable behind the walls as it is beautiful on the surface.
