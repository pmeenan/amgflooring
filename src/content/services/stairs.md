The staircase is often the centerpiece of your home's entryway. Upgrading from builder-grade carpet to a custom hardwood staircase makes an immediate, stunning impression.

### Staircase Transformations
AMG Flooring LLC specializes in complete staircase overhauls. We can match your existing hardwood floors perfectly, creating a seamless transition between levels.

- **Solid Wood Treads:** Installation of durable, solid oak or maple treads.
- **Custom Staining:** We match the stain of the stairs to your home's primary flooring.
- **Baluster Upgrades:** Replacing dated wooden spindles with modern wrought iron balusters.
- **Handrail Refinishing:** Sanding, staining, and securing wobbly handrails for safety and aesthetics.

Trust our craftsmen to handle the intricate cuts and exacting angles required for professional stair work.
