Hardwood floors are an investment that adds lasting value and warmth to any property. At AMG Flooring LLC, we specialize in laying solid and engineered wood with precision.

### Installation
Our experienced team ensures proper subfloor preparation, moisture testing, and acclimatization of the wood before installation. We handle nailed, glued, and floating setups depending on your structural needs and material choice.

### Sanding & Refinishing
Has your original hardwood lost its luster? With 20+ years of experience, Armend Gashi and our team use top-tier equipment to sand away scratches, dents, and old finishes. We offer a wide range of custom stains and high-durability polyurethanes (water-based and oil-based) to bring your floors back to life.

- **Dust containment systems** used during sanding.
- Custom stain color consultations.
- Repairs and board replacements for damaged areas.
