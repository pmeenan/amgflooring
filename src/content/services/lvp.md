Vinyl flooring has evolved dramatically. Today's Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) and Tile (LVT) offer authentic wood and stone looks combined with unmatched durability.

### Why Choose LVP/LVT?
Perfect for active households, pet owners, and moisture-prone areas like basements, kitchens, and bathrooms. These modern materials are 100% waterproof and highly resistant to scratches and impacts.

### Our Process
We begin with meticulous subfloor leveling—a critical step that many contractors rush. A flat subfloor ensures that the locking mechanisms of LVP do not break over time. Whether floating or glue-down, our installation guarantees a seamless, creak-free surface that will last for decades.

- Vast selection of wood grains and stone textures.
- Fast installation process minimizing disruption.
- Easy to clean and maintain.
